## Models
- **tw_fallen_section** (buildings; LOD0 budget 30000, `_lod1` 3000): a broken 24 m tower section lying on its side, 72 m long along X, 24 m wide and 24 m high across (about six 3.8 m floors deep, the old floor stack turned on its side). Floors are exposed: the cut end is open, you can see floor slabs, pale interior walls with soot, ripped window frames, pipes and cable runs hanging out. The long top face has broken windows and a few intact ones. Its lowest side is buried in rubble (the rubble is separate, from `rubble_field`). Origin: bottom centre of the section's footprint at the middle of its length, Z=0 at the ground line where it touches the road. The tube's lowest point sits at Z=0.
- **rubble_field_l** (props; 8000) and **rubble_field_m** (props; 4000): a rubble mound about 12 m (l) and 8 m (m) across, 2.5 m (l) and 1.8 m (m) high, of broken concrete slabs (`concrete_grey`, `concrete_stain`, `limestone_dark`), rebar sticking out (thin `rust_dark` cylinders/boxes, some bent), pipes (`metal`), bricks (`brick_red`, `brick_dark`) and a few dark `soot` chunks. Mounds are walkable: the top is a series of stepped slabs.
- **Rubble walkable hulls (colliders)**: each rubble field gets 3 `add_collider` convex wedges (labels `ramp_a`, `ramp_b`, `pile`): `ramp_a` and `ramp_b` are sloped hulls from the ground to the top of the mound on the two long sides (slope no steeper than 30 degrees), `pile` is the solid core. Colliders are named by `Model`; they are the only colliders on rubble.
- **tw_leaning_tower** (buildings; LOD0 budget 80000, `_lod1` 8000): a full 24 m Deco tower (R39 kit) baked leaning about 8 degrees toward +X. Build it from `deco._build_base(m, 24)`, 18 floors `deco._build_floor(m, 24, 0)` / `(m, 24, 1)` alternating (about 80 m tall: it must read as a real tower leaning on its neighbour, lead decision), and `deco._build_crown(m, 24)` (`m` is the model the script passes in, as in deco.py), stacked at 3.8 m each (base 7.6 m). Then rotate the whole stack about the bottom edge on the lean side by 8 degrees (`transform` with rot). The lower 2 m of the lean side is buried in a rubble wedge (the lean rests on the neighbour roof: the wedge at +X is the neighbour's roof height, 2.5 m tall, built as part of the model). Cracked base: 4-6 dark crack lines (`soot`) on the base floor and a fallen column stub at the foot. Origin: bottom centre of the un-rotated footprint, Z=0 at the ground. The lean-side corner is sunk 0.2 m into the ground so no corner floats (state this in notes).
- **Lean colliders**: one convex collider `body` covering the tilted stack (a box rotated 8 degrees with the same lean), plus the rubble wedge at +X as `ramp_a`. The base lobby is NOT re-done here: the base model collider is not needed (the leaning tower is a single model with no lobby door; say so in notes).
- **burnt_floor_overlay** (buildings; 2000; `_lod1` not needed): a flat soot and char sheet for one 3.8 m tall, 24 m wide facade module. Built on the +Y face: a dark `soot` base sheet with dripping streaks (`soot`, `concrete_stain`), black char blotches around window openings (dark boxes 2 cm proud, 1.2 m x 1.6 m), and a few `rust_dark` runs under the sills. The sheet sits at Y +0.02 to +0.05 (2-5 cm off the wall, +Y is the front). Origin: bottom centre at the facade, Z=0 at the module bottom. R40-03 scales the X by size/24 for 16 m and 32 m towers; do not make 3 sizes.
- **car_burnt_pile** (props; 4000): 3 burnt wrecks stacked and tipped, built from `jam_cars._build_car(key, burnt=True)` for 3 keys (or the car shell with `burnt` true) and rotated for a pile 6 x 5 x 3 m. Colliders: one `pile` convex hull of the stack (solid, the player cannot walk through). Origin: bottom centre of the pile.
- **ash_patch** (props; 300): a flat 4 x 4 m ash and soot ground patch, lying at Z=0.02 (no z-fight), `soot` and `concrete_stain` pieces with a few `bark_dark` charred twigs. No colliders.
