## Decisions this round follows (DECISIONS 2026-11-28, Claude defaults, owner may change)
1. **Wild park.** The reserved Park area (3x3 blocks, as it is now: NO layout change, so `world/tests/golden.rs` stays green) is a city park grown into forest. Dense trees and bushes, old paths half covered by leaves, a pond with reeds, a dry fountain, a rusty bandstand, a playground, benches, lamp posts, a statue, a park cafe kiosk. Deer or crows are optional (crows first: reuse `life` perches). **Low danger inside:** a CALM rule lowers zombie density and specials while the player stands inside the park rectangle.
2. **Stadium horde.** A big oval stadium on the reserved 2x2 Stadium group: stands, floodlight towers, four entrance gates, a pitch turned into a failed relief camp (tents, crates, a medical tent, buses). Loot inside (`metro_stadium`). **The horde is a pure count**: 30 members (`data/stadium_horde.json` `total`) that stay on the pitch by day, leave through the gates from 20:00 to 22:00, roam the avenues at night, and walk back in from 05:00 to 07:00. Only members within `activeRadiusM` of the player become real zombies, and they respect the zombie cap (`hard_cap` / `maxActive`).
3. **Stats make the horde provable:** `stadiumHordeInside` and `stadiumHordeOutside` are the pure counts (not the real zombies), so hour 12 shows all inside and hour 23 shows all outside.
4. **Park danger lower than the street:** `park_calm` in `data/spawning.json` `metro` (density x 0.4, specials -0.05, floor 0). The `metroDensity` stat reads the profile at the player.
5. The park and stadium are not climbable and have no towers (already true for reserved blocks).
