## Owner decisions this round follows (2026-11-26)
- **Detail first, minimum 20 FPS** in the slowest moments (whole game, 1080p, owner's laptop). Don't make things sparse to chase FPS. A check fails only under 20.
- **New Halden**: a NEW big dying city, about **2 km wide**, centre about **6 km from camp**, in its own area where the **swamp meets the snow**. **Ashford stays exactly as it is.**
- Towers: most 10-30 floors (40-120 m), 3-5 landmark skyscrapers ~150 m. In R38 the towers are **placeholder blocks** (real models come in R39), but heights, footprints and placement are final.
- Look: light **grey-green haze**; the skyline must be visible from far away (from the highway and from the nearest town). Nights pitch dark (no city lights).
- Reached by a **wide highway from the nearest town**, jammed with an **evacuation traffic jam** (TV-show style: cars with open doors, spilled luggage and clothes, a bus on its side, dead slumped in cars). No names or characters from any show.
- Show-level gore: bodies slumped in cars are OK; no extreme gore.
- No sounds. Low-poly flat style (palette texture), but **dense and detailed**.

## R38 shared names (every task uses EXACTLY these)
- **Module**: `world/src/metro/` (pure, no GPU). Data: `data/metro.json`. Ashford's `world/src/city/` and `data/city.json` are NOT edited.
- **Placement** (R38-01, `world/src/metro/place.rs`): `MetroSite { key: String /* "new_halden" */, name: String /* "New Halden" */, center: Vec2, half_size: f32, yaw: f32, ground_y: f32 }`. `fn place_metro(seed: u64, camp: Vec2, cfg: &MetroConfig) -> MetroSite`. Angle: halfway between the swamp and snow sector centres of `data/world_layout.json` (both read from that file, not typed in), distance `centerDistance` (6000 m) from camp, nudged by the seed (`angleJitterDeg`). Must not overlap any town (`data/towns.json`) or river crossing: if it does, step further out.
- **Layout** (R38-01, `world/src/metro/layout.rs`): `MetroLayout { site, blocks: Vec<MetroBlock>, streets: Vec<MetroStreet> }`; `MetroBlock { ix: i32, iz: i32, rect: (Vec2, Vec2), district: MetroDistrict, reserved: Option<Reserved> }`; `MetroDistrict { Edge, Midtown, Core }`; `Reserved { Park, Stadium, Hospital, Police, FireStation, ArmyLine, SafeHouseTower, Subway }` (later rounds fill these; R38 just keeps them free of placeholder towers except SafeHouseTower). Block = 64 m (one chunk); streets 12 m, avenues 20 m every `avenueEvery` blocks; ragged corners. Fns: `MetroLayout::generate(site, cfg)`, `contains(p)`, `district_at(p)`, `street_at(p)`, `height_at(p) -> Option<f32>` (flat city ground with a smooth `skirtWidth` blend to the terrain).
- **Towers** (R38-02, `world/src/metro/towers.rs`): `TowerSpot { pos: Vec2, half: Vec2, yaw: f32, height: f32, floors: u32, kind: TowerKind, landmark: bool, block: (i32, i32) }`; `TowerKind { Lowrise, Midrise, Highrise, Landmark }`. `fn plan_towers(layout, cfg) -> Vec<TowerSpot>` (stable for a seed). Heights per district from `data/metro.json` (`floorHeight` 3.8 m). 3-5 landmarks (`landmarkCount`), ~150 m, in the Core.
- **Highway** (R38-03, `world/src/metro/highway.rs`): `Highway { from_town: String, points: Vec<Vec2>, width: f32 }`; `fn plan_highway(site, towns, roads_cfg, cfg) -> Highway`, from the nearest town (Blackwater or Frostmere, whichever is closer) to the city's nearest avenue entrance. 4 lanes + median (`highwayWidth` ~22 m). Terrain is flattened under it like the R33 roads (`world/src/roads/`). Fn `highway_height_at(p)`.
- **Jam** (R38-04 placement, `world/src/metro/jam.rs`): `JamProp { model: String, pos: Vec3, yaw: f32, pitch: f32, roll: f32 }`; `fn plan_jam(highway, layout, cfg) -> Vec<JamProp>`. Mostly **outbound lanes** jammed bumper to bumper for the last `jamLength` m before the city and on the first avenues; inbound lanes mostly empty, a few wrecks. Car doors open, luggage and clothes on the asphalt, 1-2 buses (one on its side), an army truck or two, seated bodies inside some cars.
- **Jam models** (R38-05, Blender, `tools/blender/metro/`): `car_jam_sedan`, `car_jam_hatch`, `car_jam_suv`, `car_jam_pickup`, `car_jam_van` (each with doors open in a fixed pose, a dusty and a burnt colour variant suffix `_burnt`), `bus_city`, `bus_city_side` (bus lying on its side), `army_truck_jam`, `luggage_pile`, `suitcase_open`, `clothes_spill`, `body_seated` (slumped dead in a car seat, origin = seat point), `body_sheet` (body under a sheet), `highway_sign_gantry` ("NEW HALDEN" + exit arrows, no real brands), `highway_barrier`. ## Data file `data/metro.json` (R38-01 creates it, others add their own keys)
Every number in `world/src/metro/*` comes from here with `// data: metro.json <key>`. Top-level keys: `key`, `name`, `centerDistance`, `angleJitterDeg`, `sizeBlocks` (32), `blockSize` (64), `streetWidth`, `avenueWidth`, `avenueEvery`, `cornerDropChance`, `skirtWidth`, `districts` (ring sizes), `reserved` (which blocks), `towers` (per-district floors min/max, footprint ranges, `floorHeight`, `landmarkCount`, `landmarkFloors`), `highway` (`highwayWidth`, `medianWidth`), `jam` (`jamLength`, spacing, model weights), `skyline` (`skylineDistance`, tris per tower), `look` (R38-06 only: placeholder tower colours, window grid). R38-04 may add more keys inside `jam`; R38-05 adds `jam.seatOffsets`.
- `JamProp.pitch/roll` stay 0 for now (`PropInstance` has yaw only); `bus_city_side` is modelled lying down. "Outbound" = lanes leaving the city toward the town.
